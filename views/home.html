About Wabi Sabi

Wabi Sabi is an online fashion store that sells anime-inspired men's clothing, women's clothing and accessories at affordable prices. Our prints blend Japanese art with Indian motifs like lotus, mandala, paisley and peacock, and we deliver across India. Orders above ₹999 ship free, you can pay with UPI, cards, net banking or Cash on Delivery, and every product can be returned within 30 days. Read more about us or visit our fashion blog for styling guides.

FAQ

Does Wabi Sabi offer free shipping?

Yes. Orders of ₹999 or more ship free anywhere in India. Orders below ₹999 pay a flat ₹79 shipping fee.

What is the return policy?

You can return products within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange, as long as they are unused with tags attached.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, cards and net banking (via Razorpay) and Cash on Delivery.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available on all orders.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 3–7 business days.
